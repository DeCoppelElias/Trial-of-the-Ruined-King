# Challenges

Status: idea.

## What it does

Goals the player can complete during a run, such as surviving a minute without
picking up a coin, that pay out extra gold. Jetpack Joyride's missions are the
model.

## Why it is interesting

Short term goals inside a run, which the original vision names as a cure for
boredom.

## What it would touch

Run events, a challenge list and its display, and gold.

## Open questions

- How many are active at once, and how they rotate.
- How they differ from [achievements](achievements.md).
