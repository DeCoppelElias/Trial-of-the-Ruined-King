# Playable characters

Status: idea.

## What it does

Several characters the player can unlock and play as.

## Why it is interesting

Something to unlock, and variety that fights boredom. Subway Surfers shows how
well characters bring players back.

## What it would touch

The player's look and animations, the shop or unlocks, and possibly movement.

## Open questions

- Purely cosmetic, or does each character play differently?
- If they play differently, how does that fit the fairness goal?
- Not for the first version.
