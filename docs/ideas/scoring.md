# Scoring

Status: idea.

## What it does

Decides how a run earns its score. Today it is one point per completed
attack, so a six step sequence is worth six points and cancelled attacks count
too.

## Why it is interesting

Score is what players chase, so how it is earned shapes how they play.
Survival time, waves cleared or near misses are alternatives to weigh before
fixing B2 in place. See the [attack system review](../attack-system-review.md),
topic 5.

## What it would touch

Scoring, the score display, the high score, and possibly
[challenges](challenges.md).

## Open questions

- Reward survival, dodges, risk (near misses, coins under pressure), or a mix?
- Does a realm or difficulty multiply the score?
