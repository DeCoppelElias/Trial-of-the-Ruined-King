# Achievements

Status: idea.

## What it does

Milestones to reach across runs: a score, an amount of gold, or a run where
every tile of the arena carried a warning at once.

## Why it is interesting

Long term goals that reward mastery and exploration.

## What it would touch

Run events, saved progress, and a screen to show them.

## Open questions

- The full list. Every tile warned does not mean every tile is dangerous at
  the same moment, so that one stays within the fairness goal.
- Platform achievements on mobile and PC, or only in the game?
