# Gold spending

Status: idea.

## What it does

A shop where gold buys something. The candidates are cosmetics and unlocks, or
shop choices that change play as sidegrades (a trade-off rather than pure
power, such as a dash with a cooldown).

## Why it is interesting

Gold is collected and saved but buys nothing today. Spending it gives coins a
purpose, and coins spawn away from the player, so grabbing one is a risk.

## What it would touch

Saved gold, a shop screen, and whatever the purchases change.

## Open questions

- Cosmetics, unlocks, sidegrades, or a mix? Power upgrades are not ruled out.
- When to build it. The shop comes after the game is fun.
- How to keep it clear of grinding, which is a non-goal.
