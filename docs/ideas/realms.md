# Realms

Status: idea.

## What it does

A run moves through realms, each with its own environment and set of attacks,
every so many points. Higher scores unlock new or harder realms.

## Why it is interesting

It gives a run short term goals and keeps it fresh, and it gives a high score
something to unlock.

## What it would touch

The arena's look, which attacks can spawn, and what the player has unlocked.

## Open questions

- How many points between realms, and does difficulty reset on entering one?
- Do realms bring mechanical twists such as slippery floors, destructible
  tiles, hazards or obstacles?
- Is a realm chosen at the start of a run, or reached during it?
