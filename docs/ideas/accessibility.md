# Accessibility

Status: idea.

## What it does

Options such as colourblind support, a telegraph clarity setting, reduced
screenshake and speed scaling.

## Why it is interesting

The fairness goal depends on reading attacks, and not every player reads
colours or motion equally well.

## What it would touch

Settings, warning and telegraph visuals, camera effects, and time scaling.

## Open questions

- Does speed scaling affect the high score?
- Which toggles belong in the demo?
