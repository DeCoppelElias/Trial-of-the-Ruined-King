# Leaderboards

Status: idea.

## What it does

Players compare high scores with others, first locally, later online.

## Why it is interesting

Comparing scores is part of the core loop in the original vision, and it gives
a personal best an audience.

## What it would touch

Saved scores, a leaderboard screen, and an online service for the online
version.
