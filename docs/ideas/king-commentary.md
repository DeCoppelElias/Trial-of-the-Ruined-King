# King commentary

Status: idea.

## What it does

The Ruined King comments on what happens during a run: streaks, near misses,
deaths and milestones. He starts cold and wants the player dead. Across runs
he warms up the more the player keeps trying, asks why they keep going, and
over time tells his own story. Someday the arc ends and the game goes on.

## Why it is interesting

It gives an endless score chaser a reason to come back beyond the number. The
same moment can get a different line as he warms up, which fights the boredom
risk.

## What it would touch

Events from the run (hits, near misses, deaths, scores), a saved measure of
how far his tone has come, and text or voice shown over the arena.

## Open questions

- First step: comments only, with no warming up yet? Suggested, to be grilled.
- Text, voice, or both?
- What counts as keeping trying: runs played, time played, or personal bests?
- His story, and how the arc ends.
- Later: dialogue driven by AI, and more Kings with their own personalities.
